## Candidate protocol

Retrospectives may emit a candidate with observation, evidence, reuse scope, counterexample and expiry/review date. A candidate is not a rule and must not change behavior.

Promotion requires:

1. evidence from at least one completed task;
2. proof that the rule is reusable rather than task-specific;
3. a `user:` approval source;
4. placement in this index, a project guardrail or a focused Skill—not duplicate documents.

Rejected and superseded candidates are deleted; chat transcripts are never stored.

The reusable installer manages only this scaffold file. Project-promoted knowledge belongs in additional project files and is never removed or overwritten by a template update.
